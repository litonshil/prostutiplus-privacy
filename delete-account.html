Delete your ProstutiPlus account

ProstutiPlus (প্রস্তুতি+) · com.prostutiplus.app

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@prostutiplus.com from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject “Account deletion request”.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password hash, and Google sign-in link.
  • Your profile: district, division, and target exam sectors.
  • Your practice and exam history, scores, and progress data.
  • Your device notification tokens.

What may be kept

  • If you made a purchase on the ProstutiPlus website, its payment records (transaction ID, amount, date) may be kept for up to 5 years for accounting and legal purposes, then deleted.
