Privacy Policy

ProstutiPlus (প্রস্তুতি+) · Effective date: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProstutiPlus (“we”, “us”, “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ProstutiPlus mobile application (package com.prostutiplus.app) and related services. ProstutiPlus provides MCQ practice and model tests for job exams in Bangladesh.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos (other than files you choose to upload), microphone, or camera data. We do not process card payments inside the app; payments are made through your own mobile wallet or bank, and we only record the details you submit for verification.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app and your account.
  • Personalise practice questions, exams, and progress reports.
  • Verify payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Send notifications about exams, results, and account activity (you can turn these off in your device settings).
  • Send account emails such as password reset messages.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Third-party services

The app relies on the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In – optional sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) – push notifications. Firebase Privacy
  • Email delivery provider – to send account emails such as password resets.

4. Data sharing

We share personal information only:

  • With the service providers listed above, solely to operate the app.
  • When required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or ProstutiPlus.

Leaderboards or rankings may show your name and score to other users of the same exam.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to administrative tools is restricted to authorised staff. Some data (such as cached questions and your sign-in session) is also stored locally on your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Payment records may be kept for a longer period where needed for accounting, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain it by law.

7. Your rights and account deletion

You can view and update your profile information in the app at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

To delete your account and associated data, email support@prostutiplus.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”. See Account deletion for details on what is deleted.

8. Children’s privacy

ProstutiPlus is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@prostutiplus.com.
